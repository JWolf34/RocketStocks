# RocketStocks :rocket:

RocketStocks scans the whole market for stocks worth your time that you would not otherwise hear about.
It posts a small number of them per day in a private Discord server, each with a short talking point, an honest Track record and a Report of its own data.
It is informational only: it describes what happened, why it is Unusual and what followed historically, and never gives buy or sell calls.

- [docs/vision.md](docs/vision.md) is the authority for what RocketStocks is and what gets built.
- [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md) is the glossary.
- [docs/operations.md](docs/operations.md) covers deploying, the weekly Schwab sign-in, rehearsals, and backup and restore.
